ApexCare Global Health Shield - Plan Specifications (2025-2026)

Effective Date: 12/01/2024 | Version 2.1 | Document ID: DOC-POL-2025-01

Plan Overview and Annual Maximum Benefit

ApexCare Global Health Shield is our flagship comprehensive private healthcare coverage designed for individuals and families. The plan provides an Annual Maximum Benefit (AMB) of $2,000,000 for standard in-network inpatient care and up to $5,000,000 on the Elite Diamond Tier.

Covered members receive full private single-room hospitalization, zero copay for emergency surgical admissions at tier-1 network hospitals, and comprehensive post-hospitalization recovery care for up to 90 consecutive days following discharge.

Hospital Room Categories and Inpatient Accommodation

The Standard Plan entitles the insured to a Semi-Private Room (maximum $400 daily allowance). The Premier Plan guarantees a Single Deluxe Private Room (maximum $900 daily allowance). The Elite Diamond Plan provides Presidential/Executive Suite coverage up to $2,500 per day with dedicated companion accommodation.

Intensive Care Unit (ICU), High Dependency Unit (HDU), and Coronary Care Unit (CCU) are covered at 100% actual and customary charges without any internal daily room caps across all three tiers.

Direct Cashless Billing at Network Hospitals

ApexCare maintains a global network of over 14,500 accredited medical centers. For planned admissions, cashless authorization must be requested at least 48 hours prior to admission via our member portal or telephone concierge. In true medical emergencies, cashless pre-authorization may be submitted within 24 hours post-admission.

The patient presents their digital ApexCare Member ID Card and government identification at the hospital admission desk. The hospital bills ApexCare directly. The insured is only responsible for the policy deductible and non-medical items (such as personal phone calls or non-formulary toiletries).
